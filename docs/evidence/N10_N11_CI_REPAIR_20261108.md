## Diagnosis from actual failures

Node 22.23.4 had 1,452 passing, one failing and eight skipped tests; Node 24.22.2 had 1,444 passing, nine failing and eight skipped tests in the first project-test stage. Both candidate preparations stopped before packing. All failures came from the supervisor fixture file. [Verification record](N10_N11_CI_REPAIR_20261108/verification.json) retains job links, original log hashes and all failed case names.

The abort test scheduled cancellation 250 ms after entering the supervisor, before asynchronous input verification and repeated hashing of the Node executable. Both reports show no controller, empty owned groups and no signal events. They therefore describe a pre-spawn interruption with truthful `Unconfirmed` cleanup, while the assertion expected an admitted process group's cleanup proof.

The Node 24 hung-controller test exhausted its 1,000 ms work allowance while reading the supervisor module, before spawning a controller. Other expected wrapper/validation failures similarly reached the total cutoff before their intended states. The suite runs four test files concurrently; its tiny fixture budgets included process startup and executable hashing, rather than measuring only the behavior named in the test.

Separately, a fake executable wrote its ready PID before installing its SIGTERM handler. The controller could exit and trigger termination before that handler existed, making the expected SIGKILL unnecessary. These observations establish fixture phase/timing defects; they do not establish an engine regression or a general Node-version defect.

## Bounded repair and preserved guarantees

- Abort only after a controller has completed the supervisor handshake and written a valid ready PID. Require that PID in the owned group ledger, an actual signal, no remaining live controller work, and survival of the unrelated process.
- Add a deterministic abort during input-pin validation. Require no controller, child exit, owned group, launcher or signal event, and retain persisted `Unconfirmed` cleanup. Do not promote absent ownership evidence to Pass.
- Install the fake executable's SIGTERM handler before its ready marker and fail explicitly if readiness is not observed. Keep SIGKILL and actual process-death assertions.
- Allow 15 seconds total for ordinary fake fixtures, including a three-second finalization reserve, and ten seconds for interruption fixtures. Outer test timeout is 30 seconds. Context admission uses the same declared fixture work interval. These are finite test-fixture bounds only; named collector budgets, reserves, identity checks and cleanup behavior remain byte-identical.

Strict refusal reasons, wrapper acknowledgement, forbidden-spawn checks, descriptor forwarding, foreign-process survival, terminal persistence and Pass versus Unconfirmed assertions remain. Failure paths abort and await their supervisor before deleting fixtures; the readiness polling has no detached timer. Read-only reviewers independently traced the original failures and reviewed the resulting diff without finding an essential gap.
