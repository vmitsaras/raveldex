# RX-7004 — Human/device qualification handoff

Status: **Not run**. Automated browser reports are separate evidence. Fill in observer/date, OS/device, browser/version, assistive technology/version, native keyboard settings and zoom/display settings before recording observations. Do not change a screen reader's default settings merely to obtain a passing result. Playwright WebKit does not qualify Safari.

Use the unmodified complete browser or Astro directory at its actual root/subdirectory preview. Astro starts with Enable directory. The public sample has seven pages, three per result page; `walking resource` matches all seven. Guides alone matches three; Guides plus Safety matches two. Counts include the own group's active filter and do not predict what adding another OR choice will do.

1. **Keyboard and speech:** search/submit, Tab through persistent controls, open Filter resources with its summary, select Guides/Safety with Space, close with Escape/Close filters and verify summary focus. Observe meaningful loading/final/empty status speech without stale or duplicate announcements. Remove a selected chip: focus should reach the next chip, previous chip or search field. Clear all retains keyboard focus on its stable button. Reach all three pages and follow a native link.
2. **Physical composition/editing:** use a real supported OS IME with suitable indexed content. Provisional words must not be queried. Commit, cancel, Clear all, explicit Search and actual Back/Forward should preserve the newest intent and ordinary native editing. Record actual keyboard/IME versions; synthetic CompositionEvent traces do not establish this observation.
3. **Actual zoom and reflow:** use the browser's real 200% and 400% zoom at a recorded desktop viewport. Check reading order, input, long labels, counts, chips, pagination, live status and every recovery action without two-dimensional page scrolling. Verify focused controls remain visible. Viewport/text scaling from automated tests is not this check.
4. **Touch/mobile keyboard:** on a real device, open filters, select/remove choices, close to its summary and use the on-screen search keyboard. Check target spacing, scrolling and query state while the keyboard changes the viewport. Resizing must not silently close focused filters. Physical touch and screen-reader gestures need their own observations.
5. **Real theme preferences:** check OS high contrast/forced colors, dark mode and reduced motion with long/RTL labels. Preserve distinguishable checkbox, unavailable-action and focus states. The component has no animated transition; verify the application's surrounding effects too. Emulated media/selected axe passes are supporting evidence only.
6. **Recovery and history:** Clear all, select Water routes and confirm zero results retains its selected checkbox/chip; remove it to browse seven again. Exercise actual Back/Forward over query/filter/page commits. Refuse initial content/manifest/source/display loads and verify browse links/Retry remain usable. A changed native revision must clear old results and request explicit Refresh. An address-write refusal must leave search usable and offer Update search link, without another query.

For each scenario record Pass/Fail/Blocked/Not run, actual observed focus/speech/visual behavior and a reproducible failure. No human/device result is supplied by this file, and no publication/deployment follows from it.
