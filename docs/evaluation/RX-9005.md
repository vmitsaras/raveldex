## Workload and measurement boundaries

The unchanged [original machine method](RX-9001-method.json) supplies all inputs and samples: three artifacts × the same adversarial fixture at 100/1,000/10,000 records × core-only/source-and-positions × omitted 30-second/explicit 60-second deadlines × three ordinary owners plus one separate diagnostic owner. The **144 fresh processes** ran serially, with no task-owned build, test, other collector or heavy replay alongside timing.

Six single-record cases cover novel additions, genuinely changed A/B same-vocabulary replacement, novel replacement, removal, reinsertion after actual removal and disappearance of unique vocabulary. Five batch cases use B=1/32 and B=256 where N permits it. Every measured pre-population is reset to its declared state. `addMany` is one public invocation; replacement and removal are B ordered public invocations. Their results describe whole-sequence throughput, not atomic batch or single-record latency. Batch256 at N100 has **240 explicit zero-attempt Not-applicable case rows** across the matrix.

The collection has **5,544 ordinary windows, 696 diagnostic windows, 107,544 measured public calls and 72 excluded warmups**. Every selected call and window passed; there was no mutation refusal or capacity transition in these selected cases. The only p95 estimates are **12 labeled cells**, each with 120 actual same-cell successful windows: same-vocabulary single replacement at N1000 after two excluded warmups, for each artifact/configuration/deadline. No correlated sequence calls are pooled to manufacture tails.

Windows cover public invocation through Promise settlement. Invocation-to-return includes ownership/admission and the synchronous first pump; return-to-settlement is the remaining observed duration. They do **not** isolate admission, queue wait or engine-only CPU. There is one awaited call at a time, with no intentionally queued backlog. Initialization, original construction, genuine reinsertion preparation, differential resets, warmups and verification are separate records. Preparation totals cover timed public preparation calls, not every reset-diff/serialization instruction.

Sequence windows retain clocks, receipts and actual immutable root references after each successful call. Full inspection happens afterward, but this retention changes resource lifetime and may affect GC/memory pressure. Reset and verification in the same process can warm the analyzer/JIT. Results therefore describe this **observer-conditioned, reset-verified method**; they are not cold-machine or unobserved bare-mutation latency. No overhead subtraction or retained/peak heap claim is made.
