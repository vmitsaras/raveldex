These findings retain current observed limits. They do not amend the analyzer/scorer/limits, create implementation tasks, activate N5 or authorize a release. Numerical/query correctness and relevance are reported separately in the [evaluation](RX-4001.md).

| Finding | Classification and evidence | Scope and next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |

Preserve the target and scoped miss for candidate review; no speculative weakening or optional-module import is justified. |

Prior companion reports, synthetic events and process/serialized proxies do not fill those observations. |
