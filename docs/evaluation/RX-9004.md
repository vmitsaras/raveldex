## Practical limits

Use these results as evidence for the selected native Node source+phrase workloads: net wall-time gains coexist with process CPU and logical-work costs. The corpus labeled real-docs repeats 34 original sections; it is not 10,000 independent real documents or representative traffic. Three process owners on one recorded Apple M5 Pro/Node 24.21.0 host are not independent devices. Same-runtime variation, timer floors, sparse distributions, all refused/unavailable outcomes and full input identities remain visible.

No weighted overall winner, new post-N9 runtime optimization, browser/worker gain, incremental-update distribution, retained/peak heap, physical input/paint or universal platform conclusion follows. Those later selected cohorts and the final combined verdict remain unrun. Source/runtime/tests/dependencies are unchanged; this is uncommitted local measurement evidence, not fresh exact-commit CI or publication.
