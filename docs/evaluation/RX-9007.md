8 November 2026. **The selected N9 implementation has lower observed wall time than pre-N9 across the successfully matched Node query cases and most selected updates. CPU costs are mixed, some small updates regress, and recovery capacity depends on the exact corpus/options.** Accepted N9 and the measured-current control have identical implementation bytes: these findings establish no additional post-N9 engine optimization or overall winner.

## Remaining limitations and operational decision

- Keep the selected native contracts and measured gains, while retaining the documented CPU regressions and small-update wall costs. The evidence identifies tradeoffs; it does not establish a new defect requiring a speculative engine repair or a weighted overall winner.
- Qualify an application's actual schema, text volume, providers, query breadth, update mix and recovery host. A small page/`limit:0` still requires exact totals; successful ingestion or selective search does not guarantee broad queries or export/restore admission.
- P95 is descriptive and restricted:84 successful Node timing cells,48 browser wall role/case cells and 12 update role/case cells meet their actual same-cell sample requirements; separate refusal-cost tails are not successful latency. Most sparse phases/build/restore/update cells have no p95. Correlated calls do not establish a population/device tail or SLA.
- Workload repetition, retained roots/receipts, reset/verification, observer allocation and same-process warming condition the measurements. No bare-engine, cold-machine, isolated queue wait, CPU-stage cause or overhead-subtracted result is established.
- No new retained/peak heap, browser CPU, physical responsiveness, mobile/native Safari/Firefox, representative traffic or cross-engine winner is established. N11's scoped human AT/zoom evidence is separate from these performance results; it does not qualify every AT/device or change the recovery browser version.
- Optional gaps include the exhaustive recovery comparison, broader tails, deeper heap and expanded platforms. They limit claims, but are not prerequisites for this scoped conclusion under the amendment.
