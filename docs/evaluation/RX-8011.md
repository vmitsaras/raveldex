# N9 evidence review and practical conclusions

RX-8011 reviews the completed N9 measurements and their usable scope. The [independent native review](../evidence/RX-8011/native-review.md) and [independent comparator review](../evidence/RX-8011/comparator-review.md) pass within their recorded scopes. The material deadline wording correction below is applied to current guidance. Fresh local project verification is recorded separately in [RX-8011 evidence](../evidence/RX-8011.md). No new engine, capability, dependency, resource limit, model or release is introduced.

## What the measurements establish

The [native paired method](RX-8009-method.md) uses six real installed archives: untouched RX-8001, then planning, scheduling, prefix seek, ranking statistics and combined bulk restore. Adjacent differences include all preceding changes. They are conditional increments, not independent-from-baseline effects and cannot be summed. Untouched to combined is a separate observed comparison. The [alternative method](RX-8010-method.md) compares independently judged membership with foreign outputs and costs left explicit.

| Workload or change | Observed result | Scope of the conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selective positive queries | Planning narrows rare 10k evaluations from 10,000 to 11; emitted work falls from 773,073 to 12,479 | Indexed positive bounds can reduce work. Negative-only, browse and opaque predicates retain conservative paths. |
| Continuation scheduling | Common-query work, steps and yields stay unchanged while selected wall times improve | Faster continuation dispatch is not less query work, physical paint, input responsiveness or universal fairness. |
| Narrow prefix | Selected adversarial 10k dictionary visits fall from 30,007 to 75 | Ordered seek helps selective prefixes. The expansion limits still refuse broad synthetic prefixes. |
| Query-local ranking reuse | Selected common average/IDF calculations fall from 10,000 to 1 | Reused invariant statistics retain numerical facts; selected wall timings are mixed. |
| Bulk source restore | Previously refused frozen adversarial 10k source/positions configurations complete under unchanged caps | This is bounded capacity evidence for those literal configurations, not a general 10k restore promise or an equal-work refusal-to-success speedup. |
| Alternative libraries | Selected complete lexical memberships match independent judgments | Foreign scores, output materialization, retention, clauses, caps, transactions and interruption differ. There is no overall winner or equal-work ratio. |

The [paired raw evidence](../evidence/RX-8009.md) preserves adverse cells, including slower source-phrase planning, rare-query prefix paths and selected ranking/bulk timings. Only selected 100-record cells have 120 actual observations supporting empirical p95; larger six-call or two-owner groups do not acquire p95 by pooling other queries. Repeated calls within an owner are correlated, and desktop noise is uncontrolled. Do not infer 10k browser tails from 100-record Chrome calls or subtract Node/browser measurements to invent worker overhead.

## Capacity is an operation and configuration property

A successful batched build is not proof that a search, export or single-operation restore will fit. The current maxima include 10,000 documents, 256 mutations per batch, 100 hits per page, 256 expansions per group, 10M search work, 50M mutation work and 60s per-operation deadlines. These independent ceilings can be reduced, not raised above native defaults. Work and elapsed time are different budgets. The ordinary no-option deadline is 30s (or a lower configured maximum); N9 timed runs explicitly requested the 60s ceiling. The [deadline correction](../evidence/RX-8011/corrections.md) supersedes earlier ambiguous default wording; matched runs still use the same setting.

RX-8008 completes the selected frozen 10k adversarial source/positions restores in Node and Chrome direct/worker configurations, with exact prior-state/cancellation and source/offset/later-write checks. RX-8009's combined native matrix still refuses real-document/high-cardinality 10k source exports, so their restore rows remain Not run; real-document broad-prefix and high-cardinality source-phrase queries retain work refusals. RX-8010 additionally records a native high-cardinality 10k build operation deadline failure. An explicit same-input fresh retry succeeds; the original failure remains an observation and its underlying host-delay cause is unknown.

Native retrieval returns complete exact totals or a typed failure. A refusal is not a zero result or a successful partial page. Unknown worker/storage outcomes are different: lack of a reply does not prove rollback. The N9 matrices do not observe Unknown transport cases; they do not remove that public outcome or replace existing receipt/recovery tests.

## Updates and broad retrieval require separate planning

Current source maintains incremental atomic changes, touched postings/directories and revision-local state. Broad exact-total ranked retrieval may still inspect many qualifying records; requesting fewer hits does not avoid exact membership/count work. The N9 query/restore runs do not establish a new single-record-update latency distribution. Historical RX-4001 update values remain historical, with different source, data, host and method. They cannot be promoted into current latency guarantees.

Use the [practical workload guide](../support/RX-8011.md) and [packaged limits/recovery guide](../api/LIMITATIONS.md) to qualify your own schema, original text, vocabulary, analyzer, providers and host. Source/positions/snippets/UI/workers/persistence remain explicit optional imports; core does not silently retain those capabilities.

## Comparator and relevance limits

MiniSearch, FlexSearch, Orama and Lunr have useful selected lexical configurations, not a native compatibility layer. Actual versions, field/array projections, complete counts, ordered outputs and package/member identities are retained in RX-8010. Orama's extra case-sensitive original-string exact filter yields explicit mismatches. Unsupported operators/phrases and cap semantics are Not comparable. Fuse's Bitap fuzzy track and Pagefind's generated static/WASM track remain separate even where IDs happen to agree.

The 34-section technical-document corpus and 76 pre-ranking relevance judgments are small, English and incomplete. Supported-subset denominators differ; lexical engines also retain native results on their exact same subset. These observations are not general ranking-quality comparisons or representative traffic. Frozen membership labels are lexical correctness judgments, not user relevance labels. No published external benchmark numbers become local evidence.

## Remaining evidence boundaries

N9 code gzip and encoded snapshots are not retained or peak heap measurements. Browser CPU/heap, physical input/paint, human screen-reader behavior, actual zoom, native Safari, wider devices and representative workloads remain outside these measurements. Headless Chrome and Playwright WebKit are separate observations; neither transfers to untested platforms. Historical manual/platform records retain their own dated scope.

All packages remain private `0.1.0-rc.0`. Local review/test completion does not refresh remote Node22/24 CI, authorize tracking writes, pick a public version or authorize publication, tags, releases, visibility changes or deployment. Completion of N9 does not select deferred optional work.
